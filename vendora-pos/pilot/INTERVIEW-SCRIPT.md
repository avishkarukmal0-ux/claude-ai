# Owner conversation script — confirm the pain, capture the baseline, ask for the pilot

Goal of the chat (15 min): (1) confirm the three leaks are real *for this shop*, (2) capture rough
baseline numbers so you can later PROVE what Vendora saved, (3) get a yes to a free pilot, and
(4) get a soft commit to pay after if it works.

**Golden rule:** don't pitch first. Ask, listen, write down their numbers. People believe their own
numbers, not your slides. Never lead them ("wouldn't it be great if…") — ask what actually happens.

---

## 1. Warm-up (their world)
- "How long have you had the shop? Just you, or staff too?"
- "What's the most annoying part of running it week to week?" *(let them talk — note anything about deliveries, waste, ordering)*

## 2. Leak 1 — supplier credit / bad deliveries
- "When a delivery comes in, who checks it against the invoice? Every time?"
- "How often is something short, damaged, or priced wrong?"
- "When that happens, do you claim the credit back? How? …How often does it actually get sorted?"
- **Baseline to capture:** deliveries/week ___ · roughly how often short/wrong ___ · do they claim now? Y/N · rough £ they reckon they lose a month ___

## 3. Leak 2 — waste & expiry
- "What do you end up throwing away most? Roughly how much a week in stock value?"
- "How do you keep on top of dates now — memory, a marker pen, nothing?"
- **Baseline:** £ binned/week ___ · categories (chilled/bread/fresh/…) ___ · method today ___

## 4. Leak 3 — over-ordering / cash in stock
- "Do you ever run out of your best sellers? Or get stuck with stuff that doesn't move?"
- "How do you decide what to buy at the cash-and-carry / on the van order?"
- **Baseline:** runs out of top lines? Y/N · cash stuck in slow stock (gut feel £) ___ · how they order now ___

## 5. The money question (do NOT skip)
- "If something on your phone clawed back that supplier credit and cut what you bin — and showed you the pounds each month — is that worth £25 a month to you?"
- *Listen for the difference between polite ("yeah sounds good") and real ("how do I get it").* Push once: "Would you put your card on it after a free month if it proved it saved you money?"

## 6. The ask
- "I'm setting up a handful of founding shops. **First month free, I set it up with you.** If it hasn't found you more than £25, you walk away. Can we start this week?"
- If yes → book the setup (see PILOT-PLAN). If no → "What would it need to do for it to be a yes?" *(this answer is gold — write it down verbatim)*

---

## After every conversation, log it (one row in PILOT-TRACKER)
Name · shop type · the 3 baseline numbers · did they give a real "I'd pay"? (yes/soft/no) · their exact objection if no.

**Signal to watch:** you want **3 of your first ~6** conversations to convert to a booked pilot, and
the baseline numbers to be big enough that £25/month is obviously worth it. If owners *won't* pay even
after seeing the money — that's the real answer, and we pivot the customer, not keep building.

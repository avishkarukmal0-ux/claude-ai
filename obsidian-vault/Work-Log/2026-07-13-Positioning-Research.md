## Goal
Concern: "so many make POS with AI." Research the current market and define how Vendora stands out.

## What we found
- The **generic** POS end (incl. AI clones like SmartPOS AI + vibe-coded startups) is commoditising — AI lowers the cost of building a horizontal cart+payments+dashboard.
- Incumbents are payments-first (Square/SumUp) or omnichannel-brand (Shopify) or horizontal-with-contracts (Epos Now). None own the **UK convenience-store P&L + compliance** problem.
- UK c-store pain (2026): margins ~4.3% & falling, NLW → £12.71 (+£900/yr per FTE), rising theft, admin overhead, 71% independent with unaffiliated ones below benchmark.

## Decision — positioning
Vendora = **the back-office operating system for the UK independent convenience store**, not a card reader with a shop attached and not a generic AI POS. Moat = **UK statutory/domain correctness** (payroll PAYE/NI/NMW, VAT100, Challenge 25/HFSS, shrinkage, expiry markdown, margins) that AI clones can't shortcut. Beachhead = **unaffiliated independents** who lack symbol-group managed EPOS.

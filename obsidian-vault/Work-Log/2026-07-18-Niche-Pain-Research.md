## Goal
Desk-research what every Phase-1 target niche really struggles with — what they want, where they lose time and money — to design the Common Core vs Vertical Modules split and arm the field visits.

## Method
4 parallel research agents (WebSearch — the proxy blocks WebFetch), ~12 niches across Rings 1–2 + market traders. Synthesised into [[Niche-Pain-Research]].

## Headline findings
- **NEW top wedge: MTD for Income Tax (live Apr 2026).** Sole traders >£50k turnover must keep digital records + file 4 quarterly submissions. Universal, legally forced, live now. → auto-capture sales+expenses → MTD-ready export becomes a Core "must-have," not nice-to-have. This wasn't in the plan before.
- **Perishable waste = biggest untooled money leak** across all fresh niches (only ~5% of UK markdowns are data-driven — WRAP), now amplified by pay-to-bin waste law. Forecast-to-order + dynamic markdown + spoilage analytics = broadest ROI.
- **Compliance-by-design is the vape killer feature** (age+refusal log, MHRA/FSA legality flags, VPD duty+duty-stamp tracking from Oct 2026).
- **Market-trader phone-first thesis validated** — they already run SumUp/Zettle on a phone; the unmet layer is unified inventory + cash/card reconciliation + MTD. Strong Phase-1 fit.
- Universal core confirmed: MTD, shrink (~£6–7k/store), age-verification, ordering/admin drain, margin visibility, card fees.

## Decisions / implications
- Add **MTD auto-capture/export** to the Common Core feature set (was not previously called out).
- Vertical modules per niche now evidence-backed (see [[Niche-Pain-Research]] table).

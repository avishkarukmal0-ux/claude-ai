# 🔎 Customer Discovery — Finding the Common Ground

Back to [[Home]] · Related: [[Go-To-Market]] · [[Positioning]]

> **Field kit for Phase 1 of [[Go-To-Market]].** Goal: talk to 5–10 warm shops (off-licence, mini-mart, convenience) and find the **one pain they all share, in their own words, that they'd pay to fix** — then build the app around it. Read this on your phone while you're in the shop.

## The one rule
**Do NOT pitch. Do NOT mention the app.** Ask about their *life* and their *last week*, never "would you use X" — people lie to be kind. Past behaviour = the only honest data. *("How did you do it" beats "would you use it.")*

## The pain map — ranked by what the app can fix in Phase 1
Ask the question, then **shut up and listen.** Gold = where they get animated, frustrated, or resigned.

1. **Cash & carry / goods-in** (our strongest hook)
   → *"Last time you went to Bestway/Booker — walk me through it. How do you decide what to buy, and what's the faff when you get back?"*
2. **Ordering — what & how much to reorder**
   → *"How do you know what to reorder? Ever been caught out — sold out of a seller, or stuck with stuff that won't shift?"*
3. **Supplier prices creeping up → forgetting to reprice → lost margin**
   → *"When a supplier ups their price, how do you find out? What do you do about your shelf price?"*
4. **Waste / expiry**
   → *"Roughly how much do you chuck out in a week? How do you keep on top of dates?"*
5. **Do they even know their margin**
   → *"How do you know if you actually made money last week?"*
6. **Theft** *(listen for it — this is the Phase-2 till upsell, we can't solve it without sales data)*
   → *"Do you ever feel stock's just… going missing? Would you even know?"*
7. **Admin / VAT** *(credibility, not the lead)*
   → *"What's the paperwork side like — VAT, the accountant? How much time and money?"*

Always add: **"Show me how you do it now."** The notebook / spreadsheet / memory / WhatsApp-to-supplier they've built *is* the unmet need.

## Interview flow (10–15 min, casual)
- **Open:** "I'm trying to understand how shops like yours actually run day to day — mind if I ask you a few things? Not selling anything."
- **Dig:** "Walk me through yesterday — what took the most time that wasn't serving customers?" → "What's the most annoying part of running the shop right now?"
- **Then** work through the pain map above on whatever they raise.
- **Proof, not opinions:** "What have you tried to fix it? Did you pay for anything?" (paying = real pain)
- **Close + referral:** "Who else runs a shop I should talk to?" ← turns 3 contacts into 10.

## Avoid these (they produce polite lies)
- ❌ "Would you use an app that…"
- ❌ "Do you think this is a good idea?"
- ❌ "How much would you pay for…"
- ❌ Describing our solution at all.

## Capture sheet — log the SAME things every visit

## What "done" looks like
After 5–10 shops, one pain keeps coming up — in their words, emotional, and something they've tried/paid to solve. **That's the common ground.** It becomes the app's lead feature (hypothesis before we start: goods-in time + waste). Bring the filled sheet back and we design Phase 1 around it.
